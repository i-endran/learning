The Millennium Prize

The Clay Mathematics Institute has designated the P vs NP problem as one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems.

The Reward

$1,000,000

Awarded to the first person who either proves P = NP or proves P ≠ NP.

Why is it so important?

If P = NP, it would change everything. We could:

  • Break nearly all current cryptography (rendering online banking unsafe).
  • Perfectly optimize logistics and transportation instantly.
  • Solve protein folding and cure diseases much faster.
  • Verify mathematical proofs automatically.

Most computer scientists believe that P ≠ NP, meaning hard problems really are hard, and there are no magic shortcuts waiting to be discovered. But no one has been able to prove it yet.
